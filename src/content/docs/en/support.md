## Find an answer {#answers}

The [User Guide](/guide/) covers setup, including [getting a TMDb API key](/guide/#api-key) and [what to do when search finds nothing](/guide/#tmdb-connection). Its [FAQ](/guide/#faq) answers the most common questions.

## Contact {#contact}

- **Bugs and feature requests:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/samuelhe52/AniShelf/issues). Check whether someone has already reported it, and add to their issue if so.
- **Anything else, including privacy questions:** email [samuelhe52@outlook.com](mailto:samuelhe52@outlook.com). Email works for bug reports too, if you don’t have a GitHub account.

AniShelf is made by one developer in their spare time, so replies can take a few days.

## Report a bug {#bug-reports}

A good bug report includes:

- your AniShelf version, shown in Settings › About AniShelf
- your device, and its iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- screenshots or a screen recording, if you can take them

Never include your TMDb API key. It isn’t needed to investigate a problem.

## If AniShelf can’t open your library {#recovery}

When AniShelf can’t read its database, it shows **Library Recovery Required**. It keeps a copy of the unreadable files on your device and starts you with an empty library. A backup or iCloud Sync may help you get your data back.

To have the problem investigated, email the developer and attach an export from that screen:

- **Export Diagnostic** creates a short report with the error details, the app and system versions, and file names and sizes.
- **Export Recovery Bundle** also includes the preserved database, so it can contain your library and notes. Share it only if you’re comfortable sending that data.

Nothing is uploaded automatically. See the [Privacy Policy](/privacy/#recovery) for details.

## Support development {#support-development}

AniShelf is free and has no ads. If you’d like to support its development, you can leave a tip: open Settings and tap **Support AniShelf**.
